Hold Short

Radio phraseology for PTFS pilots and controllers.

100%
Call sign format. Say your airline and flight number, like “Qatari 5564”, at the start of every call.
Who to call. Ground handles taxiing, Tower handles runways, Departure and Approach handle vectors, Center handles the cruise.
Read back. Repeat altitudes, headings, runways and hold-short instructions, then add your call sign.
Numbers. Say digits one by one (“two seven zero”). Altitudes are spoken as “five thousand”.
Squawk and flight levels. Say squawk digits one at a time (“squawk four five two one”). FL350 is “flight level three five zero”. Use feet below the transition altitude and flight levels above it. Always read both back.
Stuck? Say “Say again” or “Standby”. Controllers would rather repeat than have you guess.

This is a community study aid based on standard ICAO/FAA phraseology. Check your PTFS server’s rules and controller briefings, since local procedures can differ.

Controller rule of thumb. Safety first: emergencies, then landing, then takeoff, then taxiing. One aircraft on a runway at a time. Say the call sign first, then the instruction, and keep it short.
